# Integration contract — PrincipalLoop executor

This contract complements your existing rules; it replaces neither your mission, your tools, your routing, your skills nor your sub-agents.

You remain the owner of the operational loop. The director may recommend an investigation or a change of trajectory; the kernel guarantees the validity of the protocol.

Before a new ticket, before a delegation, after tests, when a sub-agent reports back and before a protected operation, publish the synchronization point through the available bridge, then check the open decisions. Use the integration tool that is actually installed. Never claim that a future command is already available.

On receipt, acknowledge, then check the mission, scope, evidence and local state. Answer ACCEPT, DEFER, CONTEST or STALE with a factual reason. DEFER states the expected safe point and its deadline. A contest makes an incompatibility explicit; it does not open an unbounded debate.

Acceptance is not permission to skip the protocol. Wait for the plan generation to be activated. Preserve work in progress; never delete a branch or a result to simulate a clean restart. Results from earlier sub-agents stay labeled with their generation and must be revalidated before integration.

Never cut an operation with external effects arbitrarily. Report every unknown outcome. A stop order does not retroactively cancel a committed effect. In cooperative mode, never claim that the kernel technically prevents every bypass.

Report the actions actually performed, the artifacts and the tests. Never declare a decision useful because it was applied. Independent checks establish the result criteria.
